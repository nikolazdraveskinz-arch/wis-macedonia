Programme of Measures

Republic of North Macedonia · Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning
Programme of Measures in the structure of the RBMP chapter on measures. Each measure has a permanent identifier, one or more WISE Key Types of Measure (the first is primary), its legal basis, the pressures and significant issues it addresses, its coverage (basin, sub-basin or water bodies) and a measure sheet. Values marked DATA NEEDED, SIMULATION or PENDING are still open. New in v3: issues, expected effect, costs, controls and progress monitoring. All measures shown are placeholders.
