Request background documents and data

Background documents and information used to prepare the draft plan are available on request (Water Framework Directive, Article 14(1)). Most datasets can already be downloaded under Open data; use this form for anything that is not published.

Requests are handled by the Ministry under the Law on Free Access to Public Information. You receive a reference number and can follow the request below. Privacy notice
