Article 14 · public information and consultation

Have your say on the Vardar River Basin Management Plan

The plan for 2028–2033 is prepared in three consultation stages. Each stage is open for written comments for at least six months (Water Framework Directive, Article 14). Every comment gets a reference number, an outcome and a reason, published in the public log. Follow the plan downstream, from the first timetable to implementation and monitoring.
