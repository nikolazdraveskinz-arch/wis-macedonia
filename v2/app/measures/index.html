Programme of Measures

Republic of North Macedonia · Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning
Phase 1 scope: PoM as an RBMP dataset, maintained by MoEPP. Each measure has a permanent identifier, one or more WISE Key Types of Measure, the minimum information the EU expects per measure (type, coverage, status, responsible authority, cost, financing) and an indicator from the KTM indicator list. MoEPP keeps a progress record for each measure: information received from other institutions is entered by MoEPP staff with its source, and checked by the MoEPP Approver. Accounts for other institutions and reporting rounds are foreseen in a later phase.
