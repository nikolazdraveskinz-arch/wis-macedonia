Monitoring Data

Republic of North Macedonia · Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning
Phase 1 scope: monitoring data imported into the WIS. Stations and results arrive by bulk template or manual entry, each with its source and import date, and must reference a registered station (as in GWDMS today). Surface water quality comes from the UHMR Water Quality platform; the automatic API link, station status and alerts are foreseen in Phase 2.

Linked system: Water Quality platform (UHMR)

Phase 1 · bulk exchange active Phase 2 · API link planned

UHMR (Hydrometeorological Service, Water Quality Sector) runs the national surface and groundwater quality monitoring and publishes status classes on its own portal. In Phase 1, UHMR exports results and NWIS imports them with the bulk template and QA/QC. Each NWIS station is paired with its UHMR station number, so imports match automatically. In Phase 2, NWIS pulls stations and monthly results from the platform's API every night, subject to a data-sharing agreement and an API account from UHMR and its developer.
