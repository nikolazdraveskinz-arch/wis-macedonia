Users & Roles

Republic of North Macedonia · Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning
ToR Tasks 2 and 7. Roles follow the existing GWDMS set plus the two public-side roles the ToR adds (technical user and general public). Public users can now register themselves; institutional accounts are created or approved by MoEPP. Authentication can later move to an external identity provider (OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect), as the NWIS Report recommends.
