Permanent Identifiers

Republic of North Macedonia · Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning
ToR Task 3. Permanent, stable identifiers for every key RBMP entity, used as the link between spatial layers and attribute data (ToR Task 4) and across RBMP cycles. Identifiers can be created and edited only by authorised roles; they are never deleted, only retired, and every change is logged. When a water body, site, pressure or measure changes between RBMP cycles, the change is recorded as lineage (replaces / replaced by), as WISE reporting requires. The coding patterns shown are examples for agreement with MoEPP.
