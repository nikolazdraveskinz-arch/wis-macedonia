Privacy notice

Placeholder text for the mockup. The final notice is drafted by MoEPP under the national law on personal data protection.

Controller: Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning.
What we collect: name, e-mail, stakeholder category, optional organisation and municipality, your comments and requests.
Why: to manage your account, to register, assess and answer comments in the RBMP consultation, and to report on the consultation as required by the Water Framework Directive.
What is public: your comment, its outcome and your stakeholder category. Your name is shown only if you agree when commenting.
How long: for the duration of the planning cycle and six years after the plan is reported.
Your rights: access, correction and deletion of your account data; contact dpo@example.mk.
