How to obtain background documents

Use the request form, write to the contact point above, or ask at the Ministry. Permits and control measures are published in the public Water Book register.

Transboundary coordination

All four basins are shared with neighbouring countries: Vardar/Axios with Greece; Black Drim (Ohrid and Prespa lakes) with Albania and Greece; Strumica/Struma with Bulgaria; Južna Morava with Serbia and Kosovo. Coordination with the neighbouring authorities and links to their consultations are published here when available.
