What NWIS is

The National Water Information System is the Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning's central system for water data. It is developed by upgrading the existing water data management system (GWDMS) and serves the whole country: the same data model, identifiers and tools are used in every river basin district.

Purpose and aims

NWIS supports the preparation, implementation and reporting of river basin management plans under the Law on Waters and the EU Water Framework Directive (2000/60/EC).

One authoritative source

Water data from ministries, agencies and utilities kept once, with a clear owner, version and quality status – instead of scattered spreadsheets and reports.

River basin management planning

Characterisation, status, pressures, objectives and programmes of measures for each river basin district, organised by six-year RBMP cycle.

Transparency and participation

Open access to information and documents, and the three public consultation stages of Article 14 WFD, with a public record of every comment and response.

Water permits

The Water Book: issued water permits and applications in one register, linked to water bodies, with a public, non-confidential view.

Interoperability

Permanent identifiers, shared code lists, metadata and INSPIRE-based services, so that data can be combined and reused by every institution.

EU alignment

A data structure compatible with European reporting (WISE), supporting the alignment with the EU water acquis under negotiating Chapter 27.

Who runs NWIS

The Ministry of Environment and Physical Planning owns and operates the system. Data are supplied by the institutions responsible for monitoring, permits and water use; the river basin councils give their opinion on the plans. Registered users can comment on the plans of every basin.
